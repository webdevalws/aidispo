CORPORATE PROFILE

About Company

Welcome to Goel Allied Industries, where innovation meets trust in manufacturing of high-quality medical devices. At Goel Allied Industries, we are committed to patient safety and product excellence. Our state-of-the-art facility specializes in the production of sterile disposable medical products.

Proudly marketing our products under the trusted brand name "AI-Dispo", we adhere to the highest standards of quality and safety. Most importantly, we prioritize the health and safety of our workers, maintaining strict hygiene protocols and mandatory use of safety gear during production.

We are an WHO, CDSCO, GMP, CE and ISO 9001 certified company, equipped with cutting-edge automatic machinery, advanced Clean Room areas, in-house sterilization process, and a dedicated Quality Lab to meet both national and international regulatory standards.

Our team of skilled professionals, reliable access to premium raw materials, and strong financial partnerships ensure smooth and efficient operations.

"At Goel Allied Industries, quality is not just a promise... it's our way of life."
