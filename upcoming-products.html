UPCOMING PIPELINE • ISO 8669
Sterile Urine Collection Bag (2000 mL)
Medical-grade clear non-toxic PVC drainage bag with printed high-visibility graduated volumetric scale. Features an anti-reflux flutter valve to eliminate retrograde infection risk, kink-resistant 90 cm inlet tubing with stepped universal connector, reinforced hanging holes, and bottom push-pull / T-tap drainage outlet.
2000 mL Volume Anti-Reflux Valve T-Tap / Push-Pull Sterile EO
UPCOMING PIPELINE • ISO 10555
Ryle's Tube (Nasogastric Feeding & Aspiration)
Medical-grade non-toxic thermo-sensitive PVC stomach tube designed for enteral nutrition, gastric lavage, and diagnostic aspiration. Features a continuous radio-opaque x-ray line, corrosion-resistant metallic lead spheres at the rounded distal tip for smooth intubation, 4 lateral drainage eyes, and graduated depth marks at 45, 55, 60, and 65 cm. Universal color-coded funnel connector with sealing cap.
8Fr – 20Fr Sizes 105 cm Length X-Ray Radiopaque Weighted Tip
UPCOMING PIPELINE • ISO 6009
Scalp Vein Set (Butterfly Infusion Set)
Precision pediatric and delicate vascular access butterfly set. Features a siliconized, short, thin-wall surgical stainless steel needle with robotic 3-bevel lancet point for painless venipuncture. Soft, flexible color-coded butterfly wings provide stable non-slip fixation without vein displacement. Kink-resistant 30 cm transparent PVC extension tubing with standard female luer lock and vented hydrophobic closure cap.
18G – 27G Gauges 30 cm Tubing Flexible Wings Luer Lock
